Privacy Policy

Pleak · Last updated August 30, 2026

Pleak is a small workout-tracking app built for a private group of users. This page explains what information the app collects and how it's used.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and Google account ID from Google — just enough to identify your account. We don't receive your Google password or access any other Google data.

Beyond that, we store the workout data you enter yourself: exercises, sets, reps, weights, workout history, goals, and similar fitness data you choose to log in the app.

How it's used

Your account info and workout data are used only to run the app for you — to identify your account and to save and show your own workout history back to you. We don't use your data for advertising, and we don't analyze it for any purpose beyond operating the app.

Where it's stored

Data is stored in a private Google Sheet, accessed through a Google Apps Script backend that only the app's developer administers. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used by any other service.

Data deletion

To request that your account or workout data be deleted, email spelillo11@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the update will be reflected on this page with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app can be sent to spelillo11@gmail.com.
